# Attribution and scope

This is an unofficial, AI-assisted scaled implementation and experiment report based on:

- Jiawei Hu. *Lagrangian–Hamiltonian Flows for Video Prediction and Image Generation: A Symplectic Perspective*. https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35710
- Reference repository: https://github.com/Lilas-Q/Geometric_quantization_models
- Inspected commit: 979a41de542f7b190549fc94a8803a56530e4a00

The reference source was inspected as text; it was not imported or executed for these experiments. Numerical correspondence to the complete official model with shared weights has not been established. Reference source snapshots are not redistributed here. See REFERENCE_MANIFEST.json and FIDELITY_STAGE1.md for provenance and differences.

MNIST and the official Moving MNIST test file are obtained from the original download locations recorded in download_data.py and data/test_source.json. Full datasets are not included. Small fixed evaluation examples and derived prediction figures are included to document the experiment; third-party materials retain their original rights.

No new blanket software license is assigned in this publication. Public availability alone should not be interpreted as an unrestricted reuse license, or as a claim of ownership over upstream work.

Historical reports describe conditions at the time they were written. RESULTS_RECOVERY.md and runs/recovery2/summary.json are the final experiment record. The old archive manifest covers the imported recovery bundle, not newly added publication documents.
